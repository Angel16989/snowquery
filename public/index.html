Roles

Standard role hierarchy (Snowflake-style)

Creates three real Postgres roles: ACCOUNTADMIN (CREATEDB + CREATEROLE — can manage everything), ENGINEER (a regular role you assign database access to), and ANALYST (a regular role meant to be granted read-only access only). Note: this app always connects as the Postgres superuser, so you already have full access regardless of these roles — they're for controlling other logins.
